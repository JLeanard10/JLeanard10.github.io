About

I'm an Information Technology student at Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng San Pablo, majoring in Web and Game Development. Outside of class, I'm teaching myself how to design and build automations — using tools like n8n, Zapier, and Make to connect the apps a business already runs on, so the repetitive parts of the workflow take care of themselves.

I like automation because it sits between two things I enjoy: building software and solving practical problems for real people. My current projects include an AI-powered article summarizer and workflows that keep CRMs like HubSpot and Airtable in sync with the rest of a business's stack.

I'm based in the Philippines and open to freelance work and junior roles in automation and web development.
